Precision by construction

Nothing gets reported on a hunch

Every candidate has to earn its place. Before anything is promoted, SteinzDAST re-probes for an independent, reproducible signal: an out-of-band callback, a tool-confirmed exploit, a cross-identity bypass, a reproduced differential or timing result. A lone pattern match is capped at "needs review," never auto-promoted.

  • Confidence banded from the strongest real signal, not a guess
  • Pattern-only hits can reach review, but never promote alone
  • Impact has to be proven, or the severity comes down
  • Fails closed at every step, by design

Safe by design

It stays inside the lines

SteinzDAST is for targets you're authorized to test, and it behaves like it. The scope gate is enforced in code, not a prompt, and it defaults to deny. Credentials are bound to the domains you declare and redacted everywhere, so a redirect or SSRF can never carry a session somewhere it shouldn't go.

  • Scope gate enforced in code: default-deny, checked on every request
  • Credential firewall: domain-bound, never off-domain, always redacted
  • No brute-forcing, spraying, scope-evasion, or denial-of-service
  • Destructive paths (logout, reset, delete) excluded from crawling

Authenticated & multi-identity

The bugs that live behind login

The vulnerabilities that matter are rarely on the front page. SteinzDAST logs in the way your app actually works, crawls as each identity, then replays every privileged request as lower-privileged and anonymous users, surfacing a broken-access finding only when the resource is provably gated and the bypass reproduces. XSS is proven by real execution in a headless browser, not a reflected string.

  • Eight auth methods, including multi-step / MFA login, OAuth2 and mTLS
  • Cross-identity IDOR, BOLA and BFLA via authorization replay
  • Broken access reported only when it provably reproduces
  • Headless-browser proof for reflected, stored and DOM XSS
