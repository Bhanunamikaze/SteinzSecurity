Shadow IT

The assets nobody claimed

Forgotten staging servers, abandoned cloud accounts, marketing microsites, and unmanaged SaaS don't show up in your CMDB — but they're on the internet with your name on them. SteinzASM attributes every discovered asset and flags the ones outside governance.

  • Attribution that ties assets back to teams and business units
  • Unmanaged and out-of-policy infrastructure flagged automatically
  • Dangling DNS and takeover-prone records detected
  • Ownership workflows so every asset ends up with a name on it

Exposure risk

Ranked by what attackers would pick first

Not every exposed asset is a crisis. SteinzASM scores each exposure by exploitability, internet reachability, and business context — then keeps watching, so a quiet asset that suddenly opens a port moves up the queue the hour it changes.

  • Risk scoring that blends severity, reachability, and asset value
  • Change-based alerts: new ports, new services, new exposures
  • Trends that show your surface shrinking — or sprawling
  • Exports and alert routing into the tools your team already watches
