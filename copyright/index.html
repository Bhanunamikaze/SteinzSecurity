Send a copyright concern

If you believe content on this website infringes your copyright, email contact@steinzsecurity.com. Include:

  • Your name, contact details and whether you act for the rights holder.
  • A description of the protected work and your rights in it.
  • The exact website URL and material you are concerned about.
  • An explanation of the issue and the outcome you are requesting.
  • Any relevant supporting information and your signature or other confirmation of authority.

We will review the information and may request further details needed to assess it. This contact route concerns material on this website. Any notice that must follow a particular statutory process remains subject to that applicable process.
