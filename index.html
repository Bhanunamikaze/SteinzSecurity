Proven, then retested

Every finding proven.
Every fix retested.

Steiner separates candidate signals from confirmed findings. A reported vulnerability requires reproducible exploit evidence: an out-of-band callback, JavaScript executing in a real browser, or a bypass reproduced under a second identity. Stored proofs support repeat testing, so your team can verify fixes and investigate regressions.

  • Proof bundle with every finding, replayable by your team
  • Automatic retest on schedule, on deploy or on demand
  • Operator-set AI budgets; deterministic retests use no model tokens

Who we are

A security claim is worth
the evidence behind it

Steinz Security Private Limited builds security products that answer the two questions every security program is measured by: what's actually exploitable, and would we catch it? Our platform works both sides of that line: offensive testing that confirms real, exploitable vulnerabilities, and detection validation that proves your defenses hold. Everything we ship is engineered around one conviction: a security claim is only worth the evidence behind it.
