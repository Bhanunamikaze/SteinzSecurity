SteinzPurple

Prove your defenses
actually catch it

Finding the bug is only half the story. SteinzPurple validates that your SIEM and EDR would catch the attack: it plans ATT&CK-mapped tests, collects evidence per tool, turns misses into owned tickets, and proves every fix with a retest.

  • Per-tool verdicts: blocked, alerted, logged, missed
  • Missed detections become native tickets automatically
  • Retest proof shows improvement or regression
  • Evidence and a hash-chained audit trail on every outcome
