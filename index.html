Proven, then retested

Every finding proven.
Every fix retested.

Scanners flag patterns; AI-only agents give opinions. SteinzDAST reports a vulnerability only after an independent, reproducible exploit: an out-of-band callback, JavaScript executing in a real browser, a bypass reproduced under a second identity. Each proof is stored and re-fired every cycle, so a fix closes only when the exploit stops working, and a regression reopens itself.

  • Proof bundle with every finding, replayable by your team
  • Automatic retest on schedule, on deploy or on demand
  • AI applied surgically, under a hard spend cap; retests cost nothing
