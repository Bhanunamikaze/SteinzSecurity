Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit our website or contact Steinz Security.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice covers the public website at www.steinzsecurity.com, operated by Steinz Security Private Limited, Andhra Pradesh, India. Product access, customer environments and security-testing engagements may be governed by separate agreements and privacy information.

Information you provide

When you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send. This may include your name, organisation, product interests and contact details. We use that information to respond to your enquiry and manage the related business conversation.

Website delivery and security

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Requests to load pages and assets can involve technical information such as an IP address, browser and device information, requested URLs, request times and security events. These providers process information to deliver and protect their services.

Analytics, fonts and browser storage

Cloudflare Web Analytics helps us understand page views and website performance. Cloudflare describes this analytics service as privacy-first and cookieless. Its website-security services are separate and may use security cookies depending on the features applied to a request.

Some pages load font styles or files from Google Fonts. On those pages, your browser connects to Google to obtain the resources, which involves network information needed to deliver them. Other pages serve font files directly from this website.

The public site's product examples do not create accounts, take payments, upload files or run live security tests. The illustrated chat and voice demonstrations do not request microphone access or submit the example conversations to an AI model.

Why information is used and shared

Website and enquiry information is used to operate and secure the site, understand its performance, respond to requests and maintain the records needed for the resulting business relationship. Hosting, security, analytics and communications providers may process information for those functions. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Our business is based in India. Service providers may process information in other countries according to their own service terms and privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to address the enquiry and any related business or legal recordkeeping. Provider-managed logs and analytics are subject to their applicable retention settings and policies. Contact us if you need information about a particular enquiry.

You can control cookies through your browser and choose whether to send us an email. To ask about information you have provided, or request access, correction or deletion where applicable law provides those rights, email contact@steinzsecurity.com. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Security and updates

The website uses HTTPS. Please do not send passwords, private keys, access tokens or unnecessary personal information through a general enquiry. No transmission or storage method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

We may update this notice when the website or its information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Privacy questions and grievances can be sent to contact@steinzsecurity.com.
