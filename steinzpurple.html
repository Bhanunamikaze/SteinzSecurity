  • Rules of engagement, approvals, and execution windows
  • Target allowlists with egress and SSRF guards
  • One-click emergency stop across every running campaign

02 · Execute or import

Native agents, or your own logs

Run tests safely through native agents with mTLS enrollment, signed and encrypted task envelopes, and automatic cleanup and rollback. Or skip agents entirely and import structured or manual execution logs from work you've already done.

03–04 · Evidence & score

Verdicts your SIEM can vouch for

05–06 · Ticket & retest

Misses become owned work, then proof

A missed detection isn't a row in a spreadsheet. SteinzPurple converts gaps into native tickets with assignments, comments, attachments, and status events, then schedules the retest and compares initial versus retest outcomes side by side.

  • Findings and remediation tasks generated from misses
  • Native ticketing with assignment rules and retest rollups
  • Retest comparisons that show improvement or regression
  • Immutable score snapshots for every assessment
